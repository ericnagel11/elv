# Contoso Returns Policy (United States)

**Applies to:** United States customers
**Version:** 4
**Effective:** 2026-03-01

## Return window

Most items may be returned within **30 days of delivery** for a full refund to the
original payment method. The 30 days run from the delivery date recorded by the
carrier, not from the order date.

## Condition

Items must be unused and in their original packaging. Items that show wear may be
refused or may receive a partial refund at Contoso's discretion.

## Restocking fee

There is no restocking fee on standard items. Large appliances carry a 10 percent
restocking fee unless the item arrived damaged.

## Non-returnable items

Gift cards, downloadable software, and personalised items cannot be returned.

## How to start a return

Open your order in your Contoso account and select **Start a return**. A prepaid
label is issued for items over 25 USD. Refunds are processed within 5 business
days of the item arriving at our warehouse.

## Contoso Care Plus members

Contoso Care Plus members receive a 60 day return window instead of 30 days, and
prepaid labels on all returns regardless of value.
