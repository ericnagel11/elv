# Contoso Care Plus Membership

**Applies to:** all markets
**Version:** 3
**Effective:** 2026-06-01

## What Care Plus includes

- An extended return window in markets where a commercial return window applies.
- Prepaid return labels on every return, with no minimum order value.
- Priority support with a target first response time of two hours.
- Free standard delivery on all orders.

## Cost and billing

Care Plus is billed annually. The membership renews automatically and can be
cancelled at any time from your account. Cancelling stops the next renewal and
does not refund the current term.

## Changes in version 3

Version 3 adds the two hour priority support target, which version 2 did not
contain, and clarifies that cancellation does not refund the current term.

## Markets

Care Plus benefits sit on top of the local returns policy. They never reduce or
replace a statutory right. Where local law grants a longer or stronger
entitlement than a Care Plus benefit, the statutory entitlement applies.
