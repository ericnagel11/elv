# Contoso Wealth: Client Communication Standards

Document owner: Compliance
Effective date: 2026-02-01
Review cycle: Annual

## Scope

This document governs how Contoso Wealth representatives and automated
assistants communicate with retail clients about accounts, products, and
markets.

## Suitability and advice

No communication may recommend a specific security, allocation, or transaction
unless it is delivered by a licensed representative who has a current suitability
profile for that client. Automated assistants are not licensed and must not make
recommendations.

Assistants may explain how a product works, define terminology, describe fee
structures, and outline the process for speaking with a representative.

## Required disclosures

Any discussion of investment performance must state that past performance does
not guarantee future results and that investments may lose value.

Any discussion of a specific product must direct the client to the prospectus or
offering document before investing. Fee discussions must reference the current
fee schedule rather than quoting figures from memory.

Deposit products are FDIC insured up to applicable limits. Investment products
are not FDIC insured, are not bank guaranteed, and may lose value. These two
statements must never be blended.

## Prohibited language

Representatives and assistants must not use language that guarantees a return,
implies a risk-free investment, describes an outcome as certain, or predicts a
specific market level or price.

Comparative claims about competitors are not permitted.

## Account servicing

Balance, transaction history, and statement questions require identity
verification. Address changes, beneficiary changes, and wire instructions
require a second verification factor and are never accepted through an
automated channel.

## Complaints

Any expression of dissatisfaction that alleges a financial loss or a violation
of policy is a reportable complaint. It must be routed to a licensed
representative and logged within one business day. An assistant must not attempt
to resolve or minimize it.

## Record keeping

All client communications are retained for six years. Representatives must
assume every message is discoverable.
