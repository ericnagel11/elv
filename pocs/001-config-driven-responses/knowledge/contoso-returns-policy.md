# Contoso Retail: Returns and Refunds Policy

Document owner: Customer Experience
Effective date: 2026-03-01
Review cycle: Semi-annual

## Standard return window

Most items may be returned within 30 days of delivery for a full refund,
provided they are unused and in their original packaging. The 30 days run from
the delivery date shown in order tracking, not the order date.

Contoso Rewards members receive an extended 60 day window on the same terms.

## Items that cannot be returned

- Perishable goods, including food and fresh flowers
- Personalized or custom-made items
- Digital downloads and gift cards
- Opened personal care and grooming products
- Final sale items, which are marked as such on the product page

## Damaged, defective, or incorrect items

If an item arrives damaged, defective, or is not what was ordered, Contoso pays
return shipping and issues a full refund or a replacement at the customer's
choice. Report these within 14 days of delivery with a photograph of the item
and the packaging.

This applies regardless of the standard return window and regardless of whether
the item appears on the non-returnable list.

## Refund timing

Once a return arrives at the returns center, inspection takes up to 3 business
days. Approved refunds are issued to the original payment method and appear
within 5 to 10 business days depending on the card issuer.

Refunds for orders paid with a gift card are returned to a new gift card.

## Return shipping costs

Customer-initiated returns within the standard window carry a flat 6.99 return
shipping fee, deducted from the refund. The fee is waived for Contoso Rewards
members, for exchanges, and for any return caused by a Contoso error.

## Exchanges

Exchanges for a different size or color ship as soon as the return is scanned by
the carrier, so the customer does not wait for inspection. If the replacement is
unavailable, the exchange converts to a refund automatically.

## Late deliveries

If a delivery is more than 5 days past the promised date, the customer may
cancel the order for a full refund even if the item eventually arrives. Shipping
charges are refunded in full.

## Order cancellations

Orders may be cancelled without charge until they enter fulfillment, usually
within 2 hours of ordering. After that, the order must be received and returned.
