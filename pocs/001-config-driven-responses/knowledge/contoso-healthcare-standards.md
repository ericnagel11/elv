# Contoso Health Plan: Member Support Standards

Document owner: Clinical Operations
Effective date: 2026-01-15
Review cycle: Annual

## Scope

This document governs how Contoso Health Plan support staff and automated
assistants respond to member enquiries about coverage, claims, and prior
authorization. It applies to all member-facing channels.

## Prior authorization

Prior authorization is required for inpatient admissions, advanced imaging (MRI,
CT, PET), and specialty pharmacy drugs on the Tier 4 formulary. Requests are
submitted by the ordering provider, not by the member.

Standard decisions are issued within 5 business days of receiving a complete
request. Expedited decisions, available when a delay would seriously jeopardize
the member's health, are issued within 72 hours.

If a request is denied, the member and provider receive written notice that
includes the specific clinical criteria applied and instructions for appeal.

## Claims and appeals

A member has 180 days from the date of the adverse benefit determination to file
an internal appeal. Contoso resolves internal appeals within 30 days for
pre-service claims and 60 days for post-service claims.

After the internal appeal is exhausted, the member may request an independent
external review at no cost. External review decisions are binding on the plan.

## Handling clinical questions

Support staff and assistants must not provide medical advice, interpret test
results, recommend treatment, or suggest that a service is or is not medically
necessary. These determinations belong to the member's treating provider and to
Contoso's clinical review team.

Permitted responses cover benefit design, cost sharing, network status, claim
status, and the process for obtaining a determination.

## Urgent situations

If a member describes symptoms suggesting a medical emergency, the response must
direct them to call emergency services or go to the nearest emergency department
immediately, and must state that emergency care does not require prior
authorization.

## Privacy

Protected health information may only be discussed with the member, a personal
representative on file, or a provider involved in the member's care. Identity
verification is required before any account-specific detail is shared.
